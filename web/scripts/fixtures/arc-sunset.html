Arc for macOS and Windows will keep working until December 22, 2026.

We’ve decided to sunset Arc on December 22, 2026

That’s not an easy sentence to write. We spent years building Arc and celebrating each and every release along the way: we have so much pride and love for what we made alongside the Arc community.

Starting today, new Arc accounts can no longer be created. For existing Arc members, nothing changes yet: Arc for macOS and Windows will keep working, with basic updates, until December 22, 2026.

Many of the Arc features you love live on in Dia, and you can bring your Arc setup along in just a few clicks: passwords, bookmarks, extensions, history, Spaces, folders and tab groups.
